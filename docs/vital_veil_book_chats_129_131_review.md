## Characters
- **The team** (raised and trained together; mythic code names): Apollo (leader/briefer), Athena (field lead), Kratos, Loki (tracker/scout), Curie (mathematics, physics, chemistry, the outdoors), Peregrine and Viper (overwatch, drones, chemistry/biology; Viper "thrived in chaos"), Cypher (music, rhythm, tech; supplies a frequency device that "temporarily" disables), Shelby (vehicles and machines; extraction).
- **The Veil** (global crime syndicate, "new heirs"): Blaine (fifth-generation legacy) and Tessa (politics, strategy, the Veil's "window dressing", with daughter Destiny); their son Axel (#131: builds assassin droids from biological parts); Bolton and Karen (third generation; Karen trains "assets"); Claire and Maria (newest masters; Maria from a South American cartel family, Claire her champion). Jace and Simon are the predecessors they removed.

## Plot
- **#129:** Night insertion from an aircraft into the Veil's mountain valley, where an old base hides trafficking. Wingsuit/HALO jumps, a pre-placed supply crate, drone recovery, rendezvous, recon of a cleared square of ground (a hidden entrance). Grok's revision (msg 2) tightens pacing and tension.
- **#130:** Darker rewrite of the Veil's leadership scene: Karen kills a recruit who questioned her in a training duel and announces a survival tournament; Claire and Maria's rise. Grok's rewrite amplifies menace.
- **#131:** Three passes of the team briefing / insertion and the Veil heirs scene, optimised for AI audio (dashes for pauses, voice changes per character, then more character depth: Apollo's private doubt "We don’t even know what they’re trafficking", Kratos watching Apollo, Tessa's media cover story, "Assets are green. Ears and eyes open.").
