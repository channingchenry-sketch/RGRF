## 2. Earth as a shuttle: core and "vortex pressure" (messages 2–6)

| Claim | Check | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Toilets flush in opposite directions in the two hemispheres (Channing's prompt) | **Myth at that scale.** The Coriolis acceleration across a ~0.5 m bowl is ~10⁻⁵ of the flow’s own accelerations (Ωv ≈ 10⁻⁴ m/s² vs v²/r ≈ 1–4 m/s²); the bowl's jets set the swirl. Grok says the same ("Coriolis myth busted at small scales"). Coriolis does set the spin of cyclones and ocean gyres. | **Corrected** (Grounded at planetary scale) |
| Earth moves ~107,000 km/h around the Sun | 29.8 km/s ≈ 107,000 km/h. | **Grounded** |
| Inner core: solid Fe–Ni, ~1,220 km radius, ~5,200–5,700 °C, ~3.6 million atm; hcp iron | Consistent with standard models (inner-core boundary ~330 GPa; centre ~360 GPa; iron melting at the boundary ~6,200 K, Anzellini et al., *Science* **340**, 464 (2013)). | **Grounded** |
| Helical / vortex-like convection in the liquid outer core generates the geodynamo | Columnar (Taylor-column) helical flows are the standard geodynamo picture. | **Grounded** |
| "Flow reversals in the outer core … under the Pacific around 2010" | Probably a mix-up with the reported pause and reversal of **inner-core** rotation relative to the mantle around 2009 (Yang & Song, *Nat. Geosci.* **16**, 182 (2023)), which is still debated. | **[to verify]** (likely misattributed) |
| Tungsten "boils/vaporizes at ~5,930 °C"; becomes plasma at core temperatures? | Tungsten melts at 3,422 °C and **boils at ~5,555 °C (5,828 K)**, not 5,930 °C. Boiling is not plasma: plasma needs substantial ionisation (≫10⁴ K for appreciable thermal ionisation at normal density). Under core pressure tungsten stays solid. | **Corrected** |
| Core "crystalline DNA" storing memory; helium opacity hides it | Inner-core texture and anisotropy are real (seismic anisotropy, possible innermost inner core), but "DNA"/memory is a metaphor. The core contains little helium; seismology, not light, probes it. | **Symbolic** (Grok labels it visionary) |
