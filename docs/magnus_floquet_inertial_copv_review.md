## Plain summary

- **Grounded:** The Magnus expansion (Magnus 1954) and Floquet theory are standard mathematics. Channing's 4th-order Magnus runs on the Mathieu equation were rerun here: 4th order confirmed, det M = 1 to ~2–4×10⁻¹⁶, and the case run is stable (trace 0.617).
- **Grounded:** COPVs are mature aerospace hardware. NASA uses ANSI/AIAA S-081B with NASA-STD-5019A. Design burst factor is typically 2.0, sometimes 1.5 on launch and deep-space vehicles. Stress rupture is the COPV-specific failure mode.
- **Corrected:** Grok's Magnus convergence bound (2π, should be π), its accelerometer definition (specific force *excludes* gravity), its high-frequency stiffness correction (ϵ²/(2ω_m²), not ϵ²/ω_m), and its suggestion that a Magnus stepper conserves energy (it preserves det M = 1; energy and norm are not conserved in a driven system).
- **Plausible-untested:** The POL-scale Mathieu numbers, coil inductances, and coil-family comparisons are Grok-reported and still to be sized or measured.
- **Speculative:** Mapping Floquet generators onto the RGRF "inertial vectors" pillar or onto POL lift, and the metronome-style plasma-ring separation. The idea is Channing's and not yet stated [Channing to supply].

### B1. Rerun of the Magnus method on the Mathieu equation (msg 4 and Channing's scripts)

System: x'' + (w₀² + ϵ cos ωt)x = 0, w₀ = 1, ϵ = 0.2, ω = 5, 8 steps per period, 20 periods. Integrator: 2-point Gauss–Legendre Magnus, Ω = (h/2)(A₁+A₂) + (√3/12)h²[A₂,A₁]. **Grounded (standard numerical method).** Every number below was rerun on 2026-10-18 and matches Channing's runs.

| Quantity | Result |
|---|---|
| Final state, Magnus (8 steps/period) | [0.99992887, −0.01170677] |
| Final state, reference | RK45 rtol 1e-10: [0.9999284, −0.01174554]; DOP853 rtol 1e-13: [0.9999283994, −0.0117455384] |
| Max error | 3.9×10⁻⁵ |
| Convergence 8 / 16 / 32 steps/period | 3.88e-5 / 2.46e-6 / 1.54e-7, ratios 15.8, 15.9, **observed order 3.98–3.99** |
| Commutator sign flipped (control) | 2.23e-3 / 5.88e-4 / 1.49e-4, **order 1.93–1.98** (drops to 2nd order) |
| Monodromy M | [[0.30845, 0.969136], [−0.933676, 0.30845]] |
| det M | 1 − 2.2e-16 (SciPy) / 1 − 4.4e-16 (NumPy) |
| trace M | 0.6169: \|tr\| < 2, **stable**. Multipliers on the unit circle, \|μ\| = 1 |
| Regime | ω/w₀ = 5, far above the first parametric resonance at ω ≈ 2w₀ (see chart) |
| `expm_simple` vs SciPy `expm` | ~1e-16 on the Magnus step matrices; 1.7e-15 over the full 20-period run. *Caveat:* with 12 Taylor terms after scaling to ‖·‖₁ ≤ 2, the worst relative difference on random large-norm 2×2 matrices is ~1e-7, so it is exact to machine precision only for small arguments like these steps |
| High-frequency effective frequency | w_eff from M vs Kapitza √(w₀² + ϵ²/(2ω²)): 1.0000142 vs 1.0000141 at ϵ = 0.15, ω = 20 |

**Caveat (kept from Channing's run):** the state norm is *not* a conserved quantity of this time-dependent system (it ends at 0.999997 here only by coincidence of parameters). det M = 1, i.e. symplectic area preservation, is the real structural check. Grok's msg 4 line that the stepper "keeps the solution norm essentially constant … does not artificially pump or damp energy" is **corrected**: the integrator is symplectic, but energy in a parametrically driven system is not conserved and can grow legitimately inside a resonance tongue.

**Stability chart** ([PNG](images/magnus_mathieu_stability_chart.png)): |tr M| < 2 over ω ∈ [0.4, 6], ϵ ∈ [0, 2] (141×81 grid, 64 Magnus steps per period, undamped). The tongues come out at ω ≈ 2w₀/n (n = 1, 2, 3 visible), as Mathieu theory predicts. It is a coarse illustration, not a production Ince–Strutt diagram. **Grounded.**
