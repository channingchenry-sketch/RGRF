- **Merged figures.** The mycelium "1–2.9 MPa, *Ganoderma* strongest" line merges two studies. The 2.9 MPa result is fibre-reinforced *Pleurotus*, not *Ganoderma*.

The φ, ley-line and docking-station language is **Symbolic** or **Speculative**, as it is elsewhere in the repo.

## 4. Alfvén waves (message 8)

The basics are already verified in [tungsten_bismuth_core.md §7b-alfven](tungsten_bismuth_core.md#7b-alfven-alfvén-waves-the-external-ion-flow-coupling-2026-10-15): the 1942 discovery, v_A, modes, inertial and kinetic branches, the corona and Parker Solar Probe, the LAPD, Io's wings and worked v_A values. They are not repeated here. This section checks Grok's formulas and the damping list.

### 4.1 Formulas: Grounded
- **Alfvén speed (verified):** v_A = B₀ / √(μ₀ ρ), ρ = Σ nᵢ mᵢ. Grok's capture matches. Grok attaches "(ideal MHD, incompressible plasma)", but v_A does not need incompressibility. It is the ideal-MHD tension-wave speed, and the *shear* wave is the incompressible one. At very low density, where v_A → c, the displacement current gives v_A = c / √(1 + c²μ₀ρ/B₀²).
- **Dispersion (verified, with the general form):** shear Alfvén wave, **ω = k_∥ v_A = k v_A cos θ**.
  - The phase speed is v_A cos θ.
  - The **group velocity is v_A along B₀ for every k**, so energy travels only along field lines.
  - Grok's "phase and group velocity both equal v_A" is exact only for parallel propagation (θ = 0). "Waves propagate only along magnetic field lines" is right for the energy (group velocity).
  - "Non-dispersive" and "no density perturbation, pure shear" are correct in ideal MHD.
  - The compressional (fast) mode is the other ideal-MHD branch, v_f = √(v_A² + c_s²) across B (core §7b-alfven).
- **Kinetic and inertial Alfvén waves (Grok: "dispersion becomes more complex"):** Grounded. Standard forms:
  - **Kinetic** (β > m_e/m_i): ω² ≈ k_∥² v_A² [1 + k_⊥² (ρ_s² + ¾ρ_i²)].
  - **Inertial** (β < m_e/m_i): ω² ≈ k_∥² v_A² / (1 + k_⊥² d_e²).
  - Here ρ_s is the ion-sound gyroradius, ρ_i the ion gyroradius and d_e = c/ω_pe.
  - Both branches carry a parallel electric field (A. Hasegawa & L. Chen, *Phys. Fluids* 19, 1924 (1976) **[to verify]**; R. L. Lysak & W. Lotko, *JGR* 101, 5085 (1996) **[to verify]**).
  - The repo's Hall/whistler branch is in [core §7b-math](tungsten_bismuth_core.md#7b-math-hall-mhd-math-recovered-ocr-damaged), and the LAPD electron-acceleration result is in §7b-alfven.
