## 5. The integrated craft, sweeps and the Tesla-coil drive (messages 24–36)

- The craft sketch (heartbeat theta-pinch core, W–Bi–quartz cavity, Tesla-coil vortex, 60° arcs, chiral-SF feedback) has no capture: **[Channing to supply]**.
- Frequency sweeps report an "optimal 150–400 kHz" band, and "Efficiency ~60–70% (energy harvest from filament)". All are **Grok-reported**, with no model given, and are **not reproducible**.
- Energy harvest from a filament is not a known effect at any useful power.
- A Tesla coil is an air-core resonant transformer. It produces strong RF fields but **no unidirectional vortex magnetic field** and no thrust. Its RF can heat or ionise plasma, which RMF and helicon sources do with purpose-built antennas.
- The Tesla-coil "pendulum-wheel" drive design and its frequency parameters (messages 32–42) are not documented here and are omitted from the archive. As a thrust source it is **not supported**.
