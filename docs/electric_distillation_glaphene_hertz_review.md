## Key findings
1. "galphene" (Channing) vs "glaphene" (Grok) flagged; glaphene is a real 2025 hybrid, citation [to verify].
2. All three msg-4 phenomena are real; none provides lift for POL (Grok agrees).
3. Minor corrections: TE/TM fields are not purely transverse; ladder quench is source-limited, not Paschen; l = 5 drop mode reaches ~500 Hz at 1 mm.
