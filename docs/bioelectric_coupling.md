## 1. Schumann resonance

**The physics (Grounded).**
- Lightning excites the ELF cavity between the ground and the lower ionosphere.
- The fundamental is about 7.83 Hz, with harmonics near 14, 20, 26 and 33 Hz.
- **Sources:**
  - W. O. Schumann, *Z. Naturforsch. A* 7, 149–154 (1952), doi 10.1515/zna-1952-0202.
  - M. Balser & C. A. Wagner, "Observations of Earth–ionosphere cavity resonances," *Nature* 188, 638–641 (1960), doi 10.1038/188638a0.

**Biological correlations (Plausible / weak).**
- **Claims recorded:**
  - The fundamental overlaps the EEG alpha band (8–12 Hz).
  - Reported correlations with EEG, calcium signaling and cardiovascular markers.
  - Proposed mechanisms: stochastic resonance, radical-pair effects, ion channels.
- **Caution:**
  - The field strength is about pT and sub-mV/m, far below most known transduction thresholds.
  - The frequency overlap alone is not evidence of coupling.
- **Wever bunker studies:** in human isolation units, shielding from natural fields reportedly lengthened free-running circadian periods, and adding a 10 Hz field shortened them again. That is one reading of "absence as a mild stressor." I located this only through the PubMed record "The effects of electric fields on circadian rhythmicity in men" (PMID 11826883). **Full citation details are unverified, Channing to supply**, and there has been no independent modern replication.

### 1b. Schumann and telluric detail (Channing's recovered text)

**The ELF cavity (Grounded).**
- Fields are pT-level ELF, confined in the Earth–ionosphere cavity.
- They are modeled with spherical harmonics (Legendre polynomials in latitude).

**The ideal-cavity formula (Grounded).**
- For a lossless thin shell, f_n = (c / 2πR)·√(n(n+1)).
- With R = 6371 km, c/(2πR) ≈ 7.49 Hz. That gives f₁ ≈ 10.6 Hz, f₂ ≈ 18.4 Hz, f₃ ≈ 25.9 Hz.
- The observed values are ~7.83, 14.3 and 20.8 Hz. They are lower because the ionosphere is a lossy, finite-conductivity, height-varying boundary.

**Telluric currents (Grounded).**
- Natural currents flow in the crust and upper mantle, induced mainly by geomagnetic variations (magnetospheric and ionospheric current systems), with contributions from lightning and other sources.
- **Magnetotellurics** inverts the surface E/B ratio for subsurface conductivity:
  - L. Cagniard, "Basic theory of the magneto-telluric method of geophysical prospecting," *Geophysics* 18(3), 605–635 (1953), doi 10.1190/1.1437915.
  - A. N. Tikhonov (1950) developed the method independently. **Citation details unverified.**
- **Geomagnetically induced currents (GIC):** the Hydro-Québec blackout of 13 March 1989.
  - L. Bolduc, "GIC observations and studies in the Hydro-Québec power system," *J. Atmos. Sol.-Terr. Phys.* 64(16), 1793–1802 (2002), doi 10.1016/S1364-6826(02)00128-1.
  - Ground voltages up to ~1.7 V/km were measured there in 1992–94.

**Flagged:** "~32 identified mechanisms" (presumably sources or mechanisms of telluric currents) is **unverified**. I found no source; [Channing to supply].

**Diurnal patterns (Grounded):** Schumann amplitudes and frequencies follow the daily cycle of global thunderstorm centres (Asia, Africa, the Americas), and telluric fields follow the daily geomagnetic variation.

The proposed chain is: Schumann → Becker-type bioelectric fields → Fröhlich condensates / mitochondrial solitons → Davydov solitons, all modulated by light.

**The links, one by one:**
- **Bioelectric patterning (Grounded):** M. Levin, "Molecular bioelectricity: how endogenous voltage potentials control cell behavior and instruct pattern regulation in vivo," *Mol. Biol. Cell* (2014), PMID 25425556.
  - Becker's injury currents are the historical precursor. **No specific Becker citation was supplied: [Channing to supply].**
- **Fröhlich condensation (Plausible, with one contested experiment):** I. Lundholm et al., "Terahertz radiation induces non-thermal structural changes associated with Fröhlich condensation in a protein crystal," *Struct. Dyn.* 2, 054702 (2015), doi 10.1063/1.4931825.

## 4. Collagen as a bioelectric material

**Piezoelectricity (Grounded):**
- E. Fukada & I. Yasuda, "Piezoelectric effects in collagen," *Jpn. J. Appl. Phys.* 3, 117 (1964), doi 10.1143/JJAP.3.117. This is *JJAP*, not *J. Phys. Soc. Jpn.*; their 1957 bone paper is the JPSJ one.
- **Single-fibril measurement (Grounded):** M. Minary-Jolandan & M.-F. Yu, *Nanotechnology* 20, 085706 (2009), doi 10.1088/0957-4484/20/8/085706.
  - Method: piezoresponse force microscopy (PFM).
  - Result: shear piezoelectricity of about 1 pm/V, unipolar along the fibril axis, up to tens of mV under load.
  - SHG (second-harmonic generation) imaging of collagen's non-centrosymmetric order is standard, but I cite no single paper for it.

**Contested and supporting claims:**
- Proton conduction along hydrated collagen: **Plausible.** No citation was supplied.
- "Semiconductor-like" collagen: **debated.**
- EZ (exclusion-zone) water: **debated** (see `lift_and_equilibrium_systems.md`).

**Biotensegrity (Plausible):**
