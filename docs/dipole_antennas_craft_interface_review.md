## 2. Energy note

The chat gives no powers or forces, so there was nothing to rerun beyond the dipole constant. Any later sim must carry a ledger. Per #161, eddy-current lift over a conductor is bounded at about 0.15 N/W (copper, 1 kHz) and is worse at RF. A dipole radiates power away, so it adds no lift. Radiated EM momentum gives at most P/c, about 3.3 µN per kW.
