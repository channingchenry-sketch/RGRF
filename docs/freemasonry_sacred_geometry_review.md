## 4. Platonic solids, Metatron's Cube, Flower of Life [6]–[16]

- **Platonic solids [6].** The element assignments (Plato, *Timaeus*) are **Grounded** as history and **Symbolic** as meaning. The φ content of the icosahedron and dodecahedron coordinates is **Grounded** maths.
  - Correction: virus capsids and radiolaria are icosahedral, which is **Grounded**. The C₆₀ fullerene, however, is a *truncated* icosahedron, not a Platonic solid.
  - Already tabulated in [em_topological_gravity_review.md](em_topological_gravity_review.md#platonic-solids-channings-recovered-text).
- **Metatron's Cube "contains all five Platonic solids" [8].** **Correction.** Lines between the 13 Fruit-of-Life centres give clean 2D projections of the tetrahedron (star tetrahedron), cube and octahedron. The icosahedron and dodecahedron need vertices that are **not** among those 13 points, so their "extraction" is an approximation added to the diagram. Grok's own caveat in [8] goes part of the way.
- **Naming and provenance [8][10].** Overlapping-circle hexagonal rosettes are ancient (**Grounded**). The names "Flower / Seed / Fruit of Life," and the association of the 13-circle figure with Metatron, come from 20th-century New Age writing, popularised in the 1990s ([to verify] earliest use). Kabbalistic sources name Metatron but do not draw this cube. The Fruit of Life's outer ring sits at twice the inner spacing, so it comes from the *extended* hexagonal lattice, not the standard 19-circle Flower.
- **"Inner rotated at 180 degrees" [15][16].** **Correction (Grounded maths).** The Flower of Life and Metatron's Cube both have six-fold (D₆) symmetry. A 180° rotation, like 60° or 120°, maps the figure exactly onto itself. Grok's description, in which "lines cross or align in new ways," cannot be right for 180°: the overlay is identical. A visibly different overlay needs a rotation that is not a multiple of 60°, such as 30°, which produces a 12-fold moiré, or a 3D fold. Channing's origami intuition in [17] points the right way: folding, not in-plane rotation, changes the connectivity.
- **Images [12]–[16].** Channing reports in [17] that "the images didn’t work," so nothing was captured.
