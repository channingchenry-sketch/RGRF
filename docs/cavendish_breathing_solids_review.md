## 3. Einstein–Rosen bridges, Bohmian mechanics, SED, stochastic resonance (messages 15–26)

- **Einstein–Rosen bridge** (Channing wrote "Einstein-Rosenbaum"): A. Einstein & N. Rosen, *Phys. Rev.* 48, 73 (1935). **Grounded** as a solution. Traversable wormholes need exotic (negative-energy) matter: M. S. Morris & K. S. Thorne, *Am. J. Phys.* 56, 395 (1988). Casimir negative energy is real but tiny, and no known configuration supplies enough to hold a wormhole open. **Speculative.**
- **Bohmian mechanics** ("bohemian mechanics" in the prompt): D. Bohm, *Phys. Rev.* 85, 166 (1952). It reproduces standard quantum predictions and is **Grounded** as an interpretation. Grok's Einstein quote welcoming it is invented dialogue (**Symbolic**).
- **Stochastic electrodynamics** (Boyer and others) reproduces some results, such as the oscillator ground state and Casimir and van der Waals forces, but fails for others (e.g. atomic spectra). **Grounded** as a partial theory. "Static docking" using vacuum fluctuations is **Speculative**.
- **Stochastic resonance:** R. Benzi, A. Sutera & A. Vulpiani, *J. Phys. A* 14, L453 (1981). Review: L. Gammaitoni et al., *Rev. Mod. Phys.* 70, 223 (1998). **Grounded**, including in sensory neuroscience. The SR noise sweep in message 26 is **Grok-reported**.
- **Binaural tones (message 25):** Channing is right. Both tones exist physically in the two ear signals, and the single "beat" is made in the brain (superior olivary complex). Nothing at the beat frequency exists in the air. **Grounded.**

## 7. The odd planets and tides (messages 73–82)

- **Venus as a former moon of Jupiter:** no dynamical support. A body that size could not be a stable Jovian satellite, and "polarized retrograde" has no physical mechanism. Grok said simulations favour in-situ formation. Venus's slow retrograde spin is explained by core–mantle friction plus solar atmospheric (thermal) tides: T. Gold & S. Soter, *Icarus* 11, 356 (1969); A. C. M. Correia & J. Laskar, *Nature* 411, 767 (2001). Capture hypothesis **Speculative / not supported**; atmospheric-tide explanation **Grounded**.
- **Uranus "captured by Jupiters field" (Channing's question):** no. Migration in the Nice model is gravitational: K. Tsiganis et al., *Nature* 435, 459 (2005); for the Grand Tack, K. J. Walsh et al., *Nature* 475, 206 (2011). Uranus's 97.8° tilt is attributed to giant impacts or a past spin–orbit resonance [to verify which source Grok relied on]. Migration **Grounded**; field capture **contradicted**.
- **Venus tide-torque simulation (message 80):** "simplified 1D torque model", with no code or values shown. **Grok-reported.**
- **Earth atmospheric tides:** the day lengthens by ~2.3 ms per century from lunar tides (**Grounded**). The "~19.5-hour" day stalled for ~1–2 billion years by an atmospheric (Lamb) resonance is published: R. N. Mitchell & U. Kirscher, *Nature Geoscience* 16, 567 (2023), and earlier K. Zahnle & J. C. G. Walker (1987). **Plausible**, and still debated.
