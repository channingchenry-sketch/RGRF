# RGRF: honest synthesis of everything so far (2026-10-12)

License CC0. Labels: **Grounded** / **Plausible-untested** / **Speculative**. Covers every file in `docs/` plus the Resona-POL-Modular-Core simulation notes. Prepared for Channing.

## 1. The throughline
The same idea keeps coming back, in five forms:
1. **Spin / angular momentum organises structure.** Tidal torques spin up galaxies, plasmaspheres corotate, and planet–moon flux tubes carry current (R2, R8, L2). Precessing rotors and the Manta-Butterfly craft are the engineering echo.
2. **Boundaries are sheaths, and sheaths leak.**
   - The structures in this thread include plasma sheaths (Bohm/Langmuir), the magnetopause (Dungey reconnection), the heliopause "magnetic highway", cell membranes, reef crests and fungal hyphal walls.
   - The common move is *selective* exchange across a boundary (R4–R6).
3. **Charge separation is the work step.** Thunderstorm riming, the Na/K pump, mass filters and biased electrodes separate charge; a gated leak then uses the gradient (R7).
4. **Resonance as an organiser.** This covers wireless power, Schumann resonance, helium "octaves" and φ spacing.
5. **Nature as template.** Mycelium, coral, bird magnetoreception and Piezo channels are treated as designs to copy.

This spine works as a **design philosophy** (Plausible) and is largely **Grounded component by component**. It is not a unified physical mechanism. Nothing in the docs shows the five forms are one force or one law, and the strongest work in the repo treats them as analogies with numbers.

## 2. Strongest grounded pieces
- **The hover-physics simulation is the most valuable piece in the repo.**
  - It uses the Dodd & Deeds eddy model, validated against image-force and Maxwell-stress checks.
  - Lift is real, but costs ~16.7 kW to hover 88 kg, ~10 kW at best, on a conductive floor only.
  - The PID height loop works, and +10 kg costs +1.9 kW.
- **Coral reefs as wave barriers:**
  - Reefs remove 97 % of wave energy (Ferrario 2014).
  - USGS 2019 valued US reef flood protection at ~$1.8 B/yr, ~$836 M/yr of it in Hawaiʻi.
- **Mycelium material data:**
  - Insulation grade λ ≈ 0.04 W/m·K at 57–258 kg/m³.
  - Fire behaviour is measured (Jones 2018).
  - The Bath OSB insulation result (2026).
- **The heliosphere and magnetosphere chain:** Voyager crossings, IBEX, Dungey, Carpenter and Hapgood.
- **Charge-separation physics:** Takahashi 1978, Ohkawa mass filter, Hodgkin–Huxley, Skou.
- **Wireless power:** 20 kW nanocrystalline-core chargers at 98.5 % efficiency; WiTricity 11 kW pilots.

## 3. Where the speculation conflicts with evidence
| Claim | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| EM replaces gravity / "tiny gravity" (Electric Universe) | Ephemerides, lensing, GW timing; bodies are quasi-neutral, so EM cannot mimic universal 1/r² attraction | **Contradicted** |
| POL hovers at ~520 W, "high-80s–90s % efficient" | Physics model: 10–17 kW. Plate loss P/F ≥ ωδ. The "efficiency" figure was a wireless-link formula with no receiver | **Contradicted by ~20–30×** |
| Laminar precession gives lift assist | Internal rotation of a closed system gives torque, not net force (momentum conservation) | **Contradicted** (attitude control: Grounded) |
| POL mycelium frame at 420–480 kg/m³, 2.1–3.6 MPa | Published as-grown composites reach ~0.2–1.1 MPa. Densification may close part of the gap | **Plausible-untested; above the published range** |
| Re-entry harvesting powers the craft | TEG ~5 MJ (~1.4 kWh) per entry, ~0.05 % of the heat load; MHD kW–MW for seconds | **Grounded for avionics, not propulsion** |
| φ spacing phase-locks modes | KAM theory: irrational ratios *resist* locking; φ gave no lift-per-watt gain in the simulation | **Likely backwards** |
| Helium octave / element "tones" | Periodicity comes from shell closure; period lengths are 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, not a fixed octave | **Notation only** |
| Etched black-hole "informing path" | Filaments come from gravitational collapse; jets (≤7 Mpc) are younger and smaller than the web | **Speculative** |
| Earth–Moon plasma vortex | The Moon is outside the plasmasphere, has no dynamo, and is in the magnetotail only ~4–6 days/month. Io is the opposite case | **Speculative; coupling is weak and intermittent** |

## 4. Recurring patterns and gaps
- **Missing quantities.** Ideas often arrive as words ("vortex field", "informed path", "resonance node") without the variable they would change, its units or its size. The repo improves sharply whenever a number is attached, as the hover simulation showed.
- **Efficiency without a power budget.** Several claims quote a percentage instead of watts in and watts out. The rule that fixes this: always write the power ledger first.
- **Analogy promoted to mechanism.** A rhyme across scales (heliopause ↔ membrane ↔ reef) is a good prompt, but it is not evidence of a shared cause.
- **Untested materials claims.** The repo has no coupon data yet for density, compression, creep or moisture of densified mycelium.
- **Citation hygiene has been good.** Every unverified reference was flagged or dropped. A few remain open (lunar-dynamo authors, ARTEMIS paper, magnetic-window literature).
- **Honest self-labeling** is a real strength of this body of work.

## 5. Top 5 bench experiments (ranked for an average working American's budget)
1. **Mycelium coupon density vs strength** (~$50–150). Grow kit, hot press or clamped plates, kitchen scale, bathroom-scale or lever compression rig. Tests the POL frame claim directly with the existing `grow_log_template.csv`. *Highest value per dollar.*
2. **Eddy-current lift and heat on an aluminium plate** (~$100–250). Small coil, audio amplifier or ZVS driver, scale, IR thermometer. Measure N per W and plate temperature vs frequency, then compare with `hover_dynamics.py`. A pass or fail on the real POL numbers.
3. **Mini coral/reef wave flume** (~$50–100). Gutter or plastic tank, paddle, phone video and ruler. Compare wave height behind a smooth wall, a rough "reef" of rocks or 3D prints, and a porous grown block. Shows dissipation versus reflection.
4. **Resonant wireless-power link** (~$40–120). Two tuned coils, ESP32 or a cheap driver, LED or resistor load, multimeter. Measure efficiency vs distance and alignment. This grounds the "Tesla/resonance" thread in real watts.
5. **Biased-electrode sheath and leakage** (~$150–400). Neon lamp, or a small vacuum jar with a ~1 kV supply, segmented electrodes and a multimeter. Ion-saturation current vs bias tests R6/R7. **High voltage: needs safety training and interlocks.** Ranked last for cost and risk.

## 6. Most promising real-world impact
- **Grow-kit housing (best).** Mycelium insulation is already Grounded. Open protocols, a citizen-science log and housing-first placement could lower shelter cost. Structural use still needs tests and code approval.
- **Coral and hybrid reef barriers.** There is proven economic value, especially in Hawaiʻi. RGRF's role would be community restoration, monitoring and MICP or grown-substrate trials, partnered with existing programmes.
- **Wireless power.** Mature and real. The useful contribution is applying it (e-bikes, sensors, community charging), not hover.
- **Heat reuse.** TEGs and waste-heat recovery are modest but practical in homes and grow rooms, e.g. compost or grow-bay heat for curing and drying mycelium panels.
- **POL hover** is best reframed as a science demonstrator and education kit, with honest kilowatt numbers, not as transport.
