Yes — we can investigate Chronoflux.
It is an independent theoretical framework developed by Roy D. Herbert (Chronoflux Research
Initiative, UK). The core claim is that time is not a passive coordinate or geometric backdrop, but a
conserved hydrodynamic continuum whose density, flow, and constitutive response generate inertia,
gravity, spacetime geometry, quantum phenomena, and cosmic evolution.
Core Principle
The primitive object is a conserved temporal current:
∇μ J μ = 0,
J μ = ρt (nμ + v μ )
with orthogonality nμ v μ = 0. Here ρt is temporal density, nμ is a future-directed frame field, and v μ
is a spatial drift. Continuity is taken as foundational; mechanics and effective geometry are
recovered from it rather than assumed.
A minimal covariant action encodes continuity, drift energy, constitutive storage, and constraints.
Variations recover the continuity equation, a projected drift relation, a Bernoulli-like relation, the
frame equation, and algebraic constraints. Constitutive pressure arises from a density potential.
Metric variation defines the transport stress tensor.
Key Claims and Interpretations
Inertia and mass: Resistance to change in the admissible temporal flow state.
Gravity: Restoring response of an inhomogeneous temporal medium seeking transport
equilibrium. Effective spacetime geometry emerges from the quadratic response of the medium.
Dark sector: Spatial gradients in temporal density produce excess inertial response (dark-
matter-like effects). Divergence / shear tension in the large-scale flow produces negative
pressure (dark-energy-like effects). Temporal medium relaxation produces epoch-dependent
expansion rates (Hubble tension).
Quantum vacuum: Hydrodynamic ground state of the conserved temporal continuum; uniform
backgrounds recover standard results (e.g. Casimir), while gradients produce corrections.
Cosmology: The Big Bang is reframed as a phase transition from chaotic to structured temporal
hydrodynamics rather than a singularity. Modified Friedmann-like evolution and perturbation
damping are derived.
Geometric closure: An effective Lorentzian metric is recovered from the normalised quadratic
perturbation response. Full closure requires a fixed point in which an admissible state solves its
equations on a carrier geometry and reconstructs that same geometry. This remains a stated
gate rather than a completed theorem.
Recent (2026) papers on Zenodo refine the framework to a strictly four-dimensional formulation,
correct earlier projection issues, and separate admissibility, recoverability, and persistence as
distinct structures. A 2025 book, Chronoflux Principia: The Hydrodynamics of Cosmic Time,
presents the broader programme (some versions reference a fifth-dimensional hydrodynamic
substrate whose 4D projection yields the observed hierarchy).
Observational programmes proposed include gravitational-wave ringdown echoes, horizon imaging,
CMB / structure-growth constraints, precision timekeeping, and future instruments such as LISA.
Status and Honest Limitations
This is independent, self-published / preprint work (Zenodo, Academia.edu, self-published book). It
is not part of the mainstream literature and has not undergone conventional peer review. The
mathematical structure is carefully developed (action principles, covariant continuity, constitutive
relations, hyperbolicity considerations), and the author explicitly marks geometric closure and
absolute gravitational coupling as open or conditional. Ambitious unification claims (dark matter,
dark energy, Hubble tension, quantum collapse, etc. from one temporal medium) remain model-
dependent and require cross-regime calibration and falsification tests. Recent corrections show the
framework is still being refined.
It shares certain architectural instincts with regenerative and geometric frameworks we explore:
continuity as generative, geometry as response rather than primary, flow / shear / resistance as
organising principles, and a preference for deriving the hierarchy rather than postulating new
particle sectors. The hydrodynamic continuum language also resonates with plasma / Electric

plasma cosmology.

What Changed in the 2026 Core Paper
The Version 2.0 paper (16 July 2026, Zenodo) explicitly supersedes the 2022 formulation. Key shifts:
Primitive is now strictly the conserved temporal current. Continuity, stress conservation, and
Einstein coupling are no longer placed on equal foundational footing. Continuity is primitive; the
others are recovered or conditional.
Frame-flow correction. The current is now written as J μ = ρt (nμ + v μ ) with nμ v μ = 0. The
earlier one-field projection forced the supposed spatial velocity to vanish identically; that
mathematical issue is fixed.
Minimal covariant action. Variations recover continuity, the projected drift relation, a Bernoulli-
like relation, the frame equation, and algebraic constraints. Constitutive pressure follows from a
density potential rather than being inserted independently.
Geometric closure is now an open gate. An effective Lorentzian metric is recovered from the
normalised quadratic perturbation response (in 4D it fixes the full metric, not just the conformal
class). Full self-consistency requires a coupled fixed point: an admissible state must solve its
equations on a carrier geometry and reconstruct that same geometry. This is stated as
something that can be proved or falsified, not assumed.
Einstein form is conditional. It is retained as a large-scale selection result under locality,
covariance, second-order metric dependence, divergence compatibility, and the absence of an
extra independent geometric field. Absolute gravitational coupling is not fixed by continuity
alone; it remains a separate calibration or theorem problem.
ARP structure formalised. Admissibility (does the state belong to a valid branch?),
Recoverability (can earlier states or histories be reconstructed from later data?), and Persistence
(what identities and invariants survive lawful evolution?) are treated as distinct and tracked via a
Dictionary Graph / Atlas. State reconstruction is limited to attained smooth histories inside the
admissible domain; no universal reversibility is claimed across shocks, vacuum, dissipation, or
branch exit.
Related 2026 papers (vacuum response, dark sector, electromagnetic coupling, manifold structure)
follow the same pattern: they separate what continuity alone determines from what requires
additional closure conditions or empirical calibration, and they mark open promotion gates.
