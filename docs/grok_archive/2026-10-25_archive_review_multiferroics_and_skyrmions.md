Multiferroic heterostructures and skyrmions
These belong in the materials / low-power control / information corridor — adjacent to our
memristor/RF and piezo threads — not in the POL lift budget.

What they actually are
A multiferroic hosts more than one ferroic order (typically ferroelectric + magnetic). Single-
phase room-temperature examples are rare and often weakly coupled. The practical route is
artificial heterostructures: a ferroelectric layer stacked with a ferromagnet so an electric field
can write magnetic state without a large current.

Three coupling channels that appear repeatedly in 2024–2026 reviews:

1. Strain — inverse piezoelectric strain from the ferroelectric changes magnetic anisotropy /
magnetostriction in the ferromagnet. Strongest in oxide stacks (PMN-PT, PZT, BaTiO₃ +
CoFeB, FeGa, etc.).
2. Charge / orbital — polarization gates interfacial charge and hybridization; works especially
in ultrathin films and van der Waals stacks.

3. Polarization-modulated Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction (DMI) — the same broken
inversion that makes a skyrmion can be tuned by P. This is the bridge to topology.

Skyrmions are nanoscale swirling spin textures with a topological winding number. They are not
“little magnetic monopoles we can ride.” They are particle-like bits that can be created, moved,
and annihilated at current densities far below conventional domain-wall devices. In a
multiferroic heterostructure the selling point is: move or nucleate the texture with an electric
field (or voltage-controlled DMI / anisotropy) instead of Joule current.

How switching actually works
Voltage alone does not flip a stable perpendicular bit. It lowers the barrier. Then one of three
things finishes the job:

1. Precessional write — a ~100–500 ps pulse drops PMA, magnetization rings around an in-
plane bias field for half a turn, pulse ends, PMA returns, bit is latched. Pulse-width window
is brutal (picoseconds). Write-error-rate can reach 10−6 in good cells; endurance ∼ 1011
has been shown.

2. VCMA-assisted SOT / STT — voltage lowers the energy barrier so a much smaller current
can finish the switch. This is the practical near-term path (VGSOT-MRAM).

3. VCMA + voltage-controlled exchange coupling — 2025 work reports 50% reversal in 87.5
ps in an exchange-coupled free layer. Still a pillar, still a field or exchange bias in the stack.

Energy per bit: femtojoules today; attojoule talk is for 10 nm capacitors in models, not for a
hoverboard coil. Charging the MgO capacitor is the energy, not Joule heat in a write line. That is
the whole point.

Real-time 2025 measurements are the honest speed limit: the orbital response is ultrafast; the
junction takes ~1 ns to charge; magnetization then takes a few nanoseconds. Near threshold,
incubation + granularity dominate.

Yes. Pause here.

Physics and mechanics do not need a press release. Heat moves, water wants a temperature, a
loop either matches or it does not. The claiming is the part that keeps slipping — naming a
tomato an offset, naming a hose a harvest, naming a pilot a closed cycle. Resources are finite;
the sentence we put on them is not.

What remains when the slogan falls off is what you said: design, function (alone and together),
and combination. After that it is looking, trying, breaking, writing the break down so the next
hand does not pay the same tuition. The wheel gets recast because someone needed a different
axle, or because the last drawing was locked in a drawer.

A great many answers are already in the world — Odense’s pipe, a 1976 Whiteshell study,
Myera’s old museum, a 3 MW hose that has not yet published a kilogram. The work is less
invention than honest assembly and shared memory.
