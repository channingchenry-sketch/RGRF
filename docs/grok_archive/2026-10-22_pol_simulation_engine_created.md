Key Findings from Hertz’s Experiments
Hertz’s apparatus was elegantly simple yet profoundly powerful:

     Transmitter: An induction coil charging a Leyden jar (early capacitor) connected to a spark

     Receiver: A separate copper-wire loop or dipole with its own tiny spark gap. When

These results, published in 1888 as “On Electromagnetic Waves in Air and Their Reflection,”
