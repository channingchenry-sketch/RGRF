periodic time-dependent driving (like laser pulses, oscillating fields, or mechanical shaking) to

Core Idea
In a static system, behavior is governed by a time-independent Hamiltonian. When we apply a
periodic drive with period T (frequency Ω = 2π/T), the system enters a non-equilibrium regime.

effective Floquet Hamiltonian that mimics a desired static system or unlocks entirely new phases of
matter not accessible in equilibrium. This is analogous to how Bloch theory describes periodic
structures in space; Floquet theory does the same for time.
