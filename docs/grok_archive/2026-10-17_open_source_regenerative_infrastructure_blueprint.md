A Tesla coil is a resonant transformer invented by Nikola Tesla in 1891. It uses two LC (inductor-capacitor) circuits tuned to the same resonant frequency for dramatic energy transfer and voltage step-up. [Wikipedia]

Key equations (primary & secondary resonance):

[Math transcribed as plain text]

Primary circuit: Low-voltage, high-current side (spark gap or solid-state driver + capacitor bank + few-turn primary coil).
Secondary circuit: High-turns air-core coil + top-load capacitance (toroid or sphere) that builds enormous voltage via resonant rise.
