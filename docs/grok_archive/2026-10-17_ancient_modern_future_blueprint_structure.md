Plasma filaments are narrow, self-confined channels of ionized gas that form naturally in electrically conductive plasmas. They arise from the Z-pinch effect: an electric current through the plasma generates an azimuthal magnetic field that compresses (pinches) the plasma into a filament via the Lorentz force (J × B).
