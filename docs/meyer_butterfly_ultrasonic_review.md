### A2. Meyer's water fuel cell (messages 2, 4)

**Plain statement.** In 1996 an Ohio court (Fayette County Court of Common Pleas, a civil suit brought by two investors who had bought dealerships) found that Meyer had committed "gross and egregious fraud" and ordered their money returned. No controlled, independent test has shown his cell producing more energy than it consumes, and over-unity operation is not supported. Meyer died in 1998.
