## 2. Cosmology: Penrose versus Susskind
| Claim | Label | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Penrose: 2020 Nobel (black-hole formation), singularity theorems with Hawking, twistors (1967), Weyl curvature hypothesis | Grounded | |
| CCC: the conformal future infinity of one aeon becomes the Big Bang of the next | Grounded as a proposal / Speculative as physics | Requires all massive particles to lose mass (or decay) in the far future. This is an open problem in CCC |
| **CCC and Susskind's holography as "potential bridges"** | **Corrected** | They are separate programmes. CCC is classical conformal geometry across a spacelike boundary. Holography (’t Hooft 1993, Susskind 1995, Maldacena AdS/CFT 1997) encodes the bulk on a timelike or null boundary and is precisely formulated only for anti-de Sitter space, not for our de Sitter-like universe. No established link exists. Penrose is a critic of string theory |
| The Big Bang is non-localized, not an explosion at a point | Grounded | True in standard FLRW cosmology too, not only in CCC. Channing's "non localized" intuition is correct. The "big pulse" analogy is Symbolic |
| "We have shown several times that geometry can fractal exponentially … inflation" | Speculative / [Channing to supply] | No derivation is in the archive. Self-similar growth does not by itself give the nearly scale-invariant spectrum with n_s ≈ 0.965 that inflation predicts. CCC claims the previous aeon's de Sitter phase does this job, but that is not demonstrated |
| **Hawking points are "hot spots from prior-aeons black hole mergers"** (msg 4) | **Corrected** | In An–Meissner–Nurowski–Penrose (2020) they are from the **Hawking evaporation** of supermassive black holes in the previous aeon, not from mergers. | **ER = EPR** "for propulsion", "resonant shortcuts" | **Corrected** | Maldacena–Susskind (2013) is a conjecture that entangled systems are connected by **non-traversable** Einstein–Rosen bridges.
