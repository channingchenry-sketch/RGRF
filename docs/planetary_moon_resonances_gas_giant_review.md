## Key corrections
1. Io power is ~10¹⁴ W, not 10²⁰–10²¹ W (Grok's own "tens to hundreds of TW" contradicts its exponent).
2. Io orbits 4× per Ganymede orbit, not the reverse (msg 8).
3. Jupiter, not the moons, dominates the tides on each Galilean moon.
4. Trace metal escape *is* observed from hot Jupiters; bulk expulsion from cool giants is not.
