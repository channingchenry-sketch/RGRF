## 3. Graphene (msg 2)
E ≈ 1 TPa, σ ≈ 130 GPa (Lee et al., Science 321, 385 (2008)); k ≈ 5300 W/m·K suspended (Balandin et al., Nano Lett. 8, 902 (2008); later values ~2000–4000) **Grounded**.

## 5. h-BN (msg 8)
a ≈ 2.50–2.51 Å (1.8 % mismatch, computed), 3.33 Å interlayer, gap ~6 eV (Cassabois et al., Nat. Photon. 10, 262 (2016)), monolayer E ~0.8–0.9 TPa and air stability ~1000 °C (Falin et al., Nat. Commun. 8, 15815 (2017)), h-BN encapsulation (Dean et al., Nat. Nanotechnol. 5, 722 (2010)) **Grounded**. h-BN on Re from borazine, step nucleation, ~3.7 eV gap **Grok-reported** [to verify].

## Key corrections
1. 2.46 Å is graphene's lattice constant, not the C–C spacing.
2. Amorphous Re Tc ~7 K is not new.
3. Surface carbide forms on Re(0001) despite "no carbides".
