## 2. Researchers credited

- **Santiago Ramón y Cajal:** layered retina mapped with the Golgi method; the neuron doctrine. *La rétine des vertébrés* (1892/1893) [to verify edition]. **Grounded.**
- **Fritz Hollwich:** the "energetic portion" of the optic pathway and light effects on the pituitary axis (ACTH, cortisol) in cataract patients. *The Influence of Ocular Light Perception on Metabolism in Man and in Animal* (Springer, 1979) [to verify]. Historical clinical work: **Plausible-untested** as summarised, because the endocrine effects are reported, not reproduced here.
- **John Nash Ott:** "mal-illumination" and full-spectrum lighting; *Health and Light* (1973) [to verify]. The classroom-hyperactivity and tumour-rate claims are **Speculative** (small, uncontrolled studies) [to verify].
- **Robert O. Becker:** injury currents, salamander regeneration polarity, the perineural DC system and bone piezoelectricity; *The Body Electric* (with Gary Selden, 1985). Injury currents and bone piezoelectricity: **Grounded**. The perineural DC "control system": **Plausible-untested**.
- **Melanopsin ipRGCs:** Berson, Dunn and Takao (2002) and Hattar et al. (2002), both in *Science* [to verify]. ipRGC → SCN → pineal: **Grounded**. A direct pituitary modulation route: **Plausible-untested** (multi-synaptic, indirect).
- **Herbert Fröhlich** (coherent polar-mode condensation, 1968 onward) [to verify]; **Alexander Davydov** (α-helix solitons, 1970s) [to verify]. Experimental anchors Grok cites, "0.4 THz lysozyme" (Lundholm et al. 2015, *Struct. Dyn.*) and "BSA 0.314 THz" (Nardecchia et al. 2018, *Phys. Rev. X*) [to verify both]. **Plausible-untested**: still debated at 300 K, as Grok itself says.
- **Freeman W. Cope** (1973) phonon / solid-state phosphorylation model [to verify]. **Speculative** (historical model).
- **Fritz-Albert Popp** (ultra-weak photon emission). Emission exists: **Grounded**. Coherence claims: **Speculative**.
- **Fungal bioluminescence, caffeic-acid cycle:** Kotlobay et al. 2018 *PNAS*; Oliveira and Stevani (luciferin work) [to verify]. Pathway and ~520–530 nm green emission: **Grounded**. "~132 species": Grok-reported [to verify].
- **Diatom biosilica:** Kröger and co-workers (silaffins, LCPAs) [to verify]; frustule microlensing, De Stefano / De Tommasi et al. [to verify]. **Grounded** in general; the ">10× focusing" figure is Grok-reported.
