## Key findings
1. Lithium astrophysics and magnetopause physics are presented accurately.
2. Pyramids-as-field-buffers is unsupported; the energy mismatch is many orders of magnitude.
3. Internal "impedance gears" cannot propel a craft (momentum conservation).
