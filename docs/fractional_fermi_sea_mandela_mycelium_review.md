## Contradictions with the repo

- Msg 8 credits the composite with eddy-current lift. In the repo, lift is buoyancy-first, and eddy-current lift is a coil-over-conductor effect. The physics model needs ~10–17 kW for POL hover, against the Grok-reported 520 W ([ANALYSIS_2026-10-12.md](ANALYSIS_2026-10-12.md); [power ledger](lift_and_equilibrium_systems.md#power-ledger-summary)). Neither thread gives a power figure.
- Copper in living composites (msgs 12–16) conflicts with copper's fungicidal action and with the Zn/Cu fungitoxicity notes in [living_habitat_life_support.md](living_habitat_life_support.md).
- The 100 Hz–10 kHz "fungal frequency" should not be used to match coil frequencies: the native signals are below 0.01 Hz.
