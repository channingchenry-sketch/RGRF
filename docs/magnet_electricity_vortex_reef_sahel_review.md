## 1. Energy ledger (no over-unity)

| Device | Energy in | Energy out | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static attractive/repulsive magnet pair at a fixed gap, with or without piezo | none (no motion, no changing field) | **zero net work per closed cycle** | "Stable continuous energy" from static magnets: **Unsupported** |
| Magnetically coupled piezo / bistable harvester | ambient vibration | a fraction of the vibration energy (µW–mW, Grok-reported) | **Grounded**; magnets only set the preload and stiffness |
| MME harvester in a 50/60 Hz stray field | power drawn from the stray AC field | tens of mW in lab (Grok-reported) | **Grounded** |
| Pressure tiles (piezo, or Pavegen-style EM) | footstep work | ~0.2 W peak (piezo) to 3–5 J per step (EM) (Grok-reported) | **Grounded**; pulsed, not continuous |
| Rotating magnet + coil ("spin induction") | the shaft work that overcomes induced-current drag | ≤ shaft work | **Grounded** (Faraday) |
| Gravitational water vortex plant | water head × flow | a fraction of that | **Grounded** |
| Magnet motors and "free energy" devices | — | — | **Unsupported**; Grok says so explicitly |
