## Key corrections
1. ZBCP comes from sign-reversing surface bound states, not from nodes.
2. The LHC dipoles are NbTi; Nb₃Sn is for the HL-LHC.
3. Solenoid peak-to-bore field ratio is ~1.05–1.3, not 1.5–2.
4. Field shaping cannot remove the conductive-floor requirement for eddy lift.
