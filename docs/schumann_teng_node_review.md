## 2. Schumann resonances and "Harmonic Earth" [2][14]

- **Grounded.** Schumann resonances are the Earth–ionosphere cavity modes excited by lightning: ~7.83, 14.3, 20.8, 27.3, 33.8 Hz. The wavelength is ~38,000 km, comparable to Earth's circumference. Typical amplitudes are ~1 pT magnetic and ~mV/m electric [to verify typical values].
- Grok rightly says a lab-scale cavity cannot reproduce them [14]. A local 7.83 Hz signal generator is just a generator.
- **Biological entrainment at 7.83 Hz [2].** **Plausible-untested.** EEG alpha/theta overlap is a coincidence of band; health claims are unsupported, and Grok flags this.

## 4. Hexagrammic TENG node [6]–[12]

- **TENGs.** **Grounded.** Triboelectric nanogenerators (Z. L. Wang group, from 2012) harvest low-frequency motion at high voltage and low current, typically µW–mW per device.
- **"Two golden triangles like Star of David" [5][6].** **Correction (geometry).** A hexagram is two *equilateral* triangles (60°). Golden triangles (72-72-36) cannot interlock into a regular hexagram. Grok's "retain the 72°/36° phi essence at key vertices" glosses over this. The two are different figures.
- **Membrane "simulation" [10].** Rerun §1.
  - With Grok's T = 200 N/m and σ = 5 kg/m², the circular-membrane fundamental is 4.84 Hz at a = 0.5 m and 8.07 Hz at a = 0.3 m. Grok's numbers are correct for that simple model.
  - **Internal contradiction.** Grok then says golden-spiral paths "increase effective wave-travel length by φ, which lowers the resonant frequency proportionally," but the φ factor was not applied. With it, the values would be 2.99 and 4.99 Hz.
  - **"Sympathetic coupling" with Schumann fields** is **not supported**. The EM pressure of Schumann fields is ~10⁻¹⁸ Pa (rerun §2), so there is no mechanical drive. A membrane tuned to 7.83 Hz responds to wind and footsteps, not to the Earth–ionosphere cavity.
- **Rigid gear + flexible triangular wheel [11][12].** **Plausible-untested** as a mechanical harvester layout: a drive gear plus compliant contact-separation segments.

## 5. Active Schumann resonator node: copper tube, nitinol muscle, bubbles, ferrofluid, helium loops [15]–[24]

- **"Electric static standing waves" in a copper tube at 7.83 Hz [15][16].** **Not supported.** A standing EM wave at 7.83 Hz needs a structure about the size of the planet. A tube can carry a quasi-static field, but not a standing wave. Grok concedes this partly in [16].
- **Bubble dynamics [18].** **Grounded:** Rayleigh–Plesset equation (form shown, prefactors [MATH garbled]).
- **Sonoluminescence [20].** **Grounded:** single-bubble SL needs ~20–40 kHz acoustic drive. At a ~7.83 Hz mechanical drive, expect cavitation only from local high-speed flow, not SL. Grok's "occasional flashes" is **Speculative**.
- **Ferrofluid or MR fluid through the tube [22].** **Grounded:** field-dependent viscosity and magnetic pumping. The "Schumann coupling" tuning is **Speculative**, as in §4.
- **Two helium loops intersecting at the top and bottom of the tube [23][24].** **Plausible-untested** for cooling. They are only needed if something gets hot; see the helium-vs-water conductivity note in [nitinol_buga_resonant_node_review.md §2](nitinol_buga_resonant_node_review.md#2-nitinol-24).

## 6. Superconducting shielding and gamma [26]–[30]

- **Meissner shielding [26].** **Grounded:** HTS shields expel flux below H_c1 and need cryogenics.
- **Correction.** Grok says mu-metal shields "saturate and lose effectiveness at low frequencies." Mu-metal is the *standard* DC/ELF shield. It saturates at high field *amplitude*, not at low frequency. A superconducting shield is far more expensive and only worth it for extreme field rejection.
- **TGFs [28][30].** **Grounded:** terrestrial gamma-ray flashes were discovered by CGRO/BATSE in 1994 (Fishman et al., *Science* 264, 1313, doi:10.1126/science.264.5163.1313). They are linked to lightning, as Schumann resonances are.
- **Correction.** Most TGFs originate at ~10–15 km altitude and are beamed *upward*, which is why they are seen from orbit. Ground-level detections are rare (some winter-thunderstorm and downward events). A ground node would almost never see one.
- **"Gamma attractor" [29][30].** **Not supported** as a concept. Nothing "attracts" photons. High-Z material only raises the interaction (absorption) probability, which is shielding. Grok says "There is no simple passive attractor"; the "phi-spiral funnel" is **Symbolic**.
