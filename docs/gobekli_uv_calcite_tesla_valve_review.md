## 3. Calcite, lime and UV [4]–[10]

- **Fluorescence [4].** **Grounded.**
  - Calcite fluorescence is mostly activator-driven (Mn²⁺ red-orange; organics blue-white). Pure calcite is weak. Shortwave (254 nm) vs. longwave (365 nm) responses vary by specimen.
  - Archaeologists use UV to reveal pigments and residues.
  - The claim that the Göbekli limestone "would almost certainly fluoresce" is plausible but [to verify] on site samples.
- **UV-induced defects [4].** CO₃⁻ and CO₃³⁻ radical centres from ionising or UV irradiation are **Grounded**; EPR dating uses them. "Modest UV doses" creating significant defects is [to verify]. Most such centres need ionising radiation.
- **Lime [3][4].** The CaCO₃ → CaO → Ca(OH)₂ → CaCO₃ lime cycle is **Grounded**.
- **Calcite piezoelectricity and SHG [6][8].**
  - **Grounded:** calcite (R3̄c) is centrosymmetric. Bulk piezoelectricity and bulk SHG are symmetry-forbidden, and Grok says so in [8].
  - Observed SHG in pineal microcrystals comes from surface or defect symmetry breaking. Baconnier et al., *Bioelectromagnetics* 23, 488 (2002), doi:10.1002/bem.10053, identified the pineal microcrystals as calcite and *proposed* an electromechanical role, which is **Speculative**.
  - Channing's "more sensitive detector" idea [5] runs into this: a symmetry-forbidden response cannot make a more sensitive transducer than quartz or PZT. **Not supported** for bulk calcite.
- **Limestone as a damper [5][6].** Porous limestone has moderate damping. Seismic isolation uses elastomeric or friction bearings, not limestone mass, so a limestone-rich matrix as a vibration damper is **Plausible-untested** at best.
- **SHG vs. "sympathetic resonance" [7][8].** SHG is a nonlinear *optical* process (χ⁽²⁾). Sympathetic resonance is linear *mechanical* coupling at a shared frequency. Both involve frequency relations, but they are different phenomena. Grok's distinction is **Grounded**.
- **Melatonin and light [10].**
  - **Grounded:** ipRGC melanopsin (peak ~480 nm) → retinohypothalamic tract → SCN → pineal, so light suppresses melatonin. The mammalian pineal is not directly photosensitive.
  - Pineal calcite "supporting healthy circadian entrainment" is **Speculative**: pineal calcification is generally age-related and not shown to be functional.

- **Grounded:** dawn chorus timing and its main drivers (territory, mate attraction), and 1–8 kHz song bands. Grok says this too.
- **"Birds singing to prepare vegetation to receive morning sun."** **Plausible-untested** as a hypothesis, with no direct evidence. ## 5. Tesla valves [14][22]

- **Grounded:** US Patent 1,329,559 (Tesla, 1920), a fixed-geometry fluidic diode.
- **Correction (diodicity).** Grok says "diodicity … can reach 15–40×." Measurements on Tesla's geometry give diodicity ≈ 1 below Re ~100, rising to **~2** for Re ~300–1500. Pulsatile forcing improves rectification several-fold. Source: Nguyen, Abouezzi & Ristroph, *Nat. Commun.* 12, 2884 (2021), doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23009-y. Multistage valves raise the overall ratio.
- **Use in the HOOH life-support loop [22].** **Plausible-untested** as a no-moving-parts backflow resistor. It is a resistor, not a seal, so it does not replace a check valve where zero backflow matters.

## 6. Plasma arcs, cyclotron-magnetron loops, HOOH and gyroscopes [16]–[20]

- **Tesla-valve geometry steering a plasma arc [15][16].** **Speculative.** Arcs follow current paths set by electrodes, gas flow and fields; gas-sheath and magnetic steering are the **Grounded** methods. A fluidic-diode geometry might bias a gas-stabilised arc, but there is no data.
- **"Two loops that feed each other" [17][18].** Coupled loops can exchange energy, but each loop has losses, so the pair needs continuous input. A self-feeding pair would be perpetual motion. **Not supported** as self-sustaining.
- **HOOH ionisation loop [19][20].** Catalytic H₂O₂ decomposition to O₂ for life support is **Grounded**: it has been proposed and used for closed-environment oxygen supply [to verify]. Plasma ionisation of HOOH vapour is **Speculative** and adds hazard. Concentrated peroxide is a fire and explosion risk with organics.
- **"Gyroscopes once spinning can be lifted slightly in any direction to travel" [19].** **Correction (Grounded mechanics).** A spinning gyroscope resists changes to its spin *axis*: torque produces precession. It produces no net force and cannot lift or propel itself; "reactionless" gyro-drive claims (e.g., Laithwaite's 1974 demonstrations) have not survived testing. A gyro can stabilise attitude only.
