## 7. What holds up, in one paragraph

The geometry is solid once two slips are fixed: the vertex list mixes orientations, and the VE has 12 radial vectors, not 24. The quanta-module volumes check out; the E-module formula lost a √2/8 factor in capture. The icosahedral jitterbug phase is a single 22.24° point. Tensegrity mechanics as Grok states them are correct, and phased transducer arrays and travelling-wave motors are a real way to get "circular motion" from back-and-forth elements. The vortex-node energy story does not hold. Quartz sets frequency but gives no gain. Nitinol needs no cryogen and is heated by changing fields, not static magnets. Nitinol amplifies force, not energy. No configuration "stays in motion" without continuous input. Entanglement cannot operate in the node. Eddy-current hover stays in the repo's ~10–17 kW regime, not the Grok-reported 520 W.
