## Where the map diverges from physics
- **Charge sign is not tied to E versus B.** Electric charge comes in both signs. "Negative" is a historical convention (Franklin), not a property of the electric field.
- **Magnetism has no "positive".** No magnetic monopoles have been observed (∇·B = 0). Field lines close on themselves, and north/south poles always come in pairs.
- **E and B are frames of one field.** They are components of a single electromagnetic field tensor F^μν. What one observer calls purely electric, a moving observer sees partly as magnetic. "Inward" versus "outward" is not a fixed property of either.
- **Grounded parts:**
  - Standing waves, nodal geometries and interference in a medium follow the wave equation (Chladni plates, acoustic levitation, cavity modes). **Grounded.**
  - Light as a propagating EM oscillation: **Grounded**.
  - "Sound" in the same row as light: an **analogy** only. Sound is a mechanical pressure wave; light needs no medium.
- **Platonic solids / golden geometry:** these appear in crystals, quasicrystals and phyllotaxis. As causes of EM behaviour they are **Speculative** (see README: the phi spiral tested worse in the POL sim).
- **"Subtle medium / information field":** **Speculative.** No luminiferous ether exists (Michelson–Morley, special relativity).

## Blue light, magnetism & biological switches
- **Radical-pair magnetoreception (Grounded theory, Plausible in vivo).**
  - Ritz, Adem & Schulten, "A model for photoreceptor-based magnetoreception in birds", *Biophysical Journal* 78(2):707–718 (2000), doi 10.1016/S0006-3495(00)76629-X. It proposed light-induced radical pairs in cryptochrome as the bird compass.
  - Xu et al., "Magnetic sensitivity of cryptochrome 4 from a migratory songbird", *Nature* 594:535–540 (2021). European robin CRY4 is magnetically sensitive *in vitro*, more so than chicken or pigeon CRY4, via flavin–tryptophan radical pairs formed under **blue-light** photoreduction. The authors note that in-vivo proof is still needed.
- **Mechanosensitive switches (Grounded).** Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2021 to David Julius and Ardem Patapoutian, "for their discoveries of receptors for temperature and touch". PIEZO1 and PIEZO2 are pressure-activated ion channels; PIEZO2 underlies touch and proprioception.
- **Electrical switches (Grounded).** Voltage-gated Na⁺/K⁺/Ca²⁺ channels generate action potentials (textbook neurophysiology).
- **Optogenetics: a blue-light switch (Grounded).** Boyden, Zhang, Bamberg, Nagel & Deisseroth, "Millisecond-timescale, genetically targeted optical control of neural activity", *Nature Neuroscience* 8:1263–1268 (2005). Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR2) opens under blue light (~470 nm) and fires neurons with millisecond precision.
- **RGRF link (Speculative / creative):** blue light as a "switching" band that couples light, spin and biology. The documented couplings are molecular (radical pairs, opsins), not field-scale.

## Birds: UV vision, calls & dialects
- **UV vision (Grounded).** Most birds are tetrachromats, with four cone types including a UV- or violet-sensitive cone. They see plumage and food patterns invisible to humans (textbook sensory ecology).
- **Song dialects (Grounded).**
  - Marler & Tamura, "Song 'dialects' in three populations of white-crowned sparrows", *The Condor* 64(5):368 (1962). Songs were stable within populations and different between them.
  - Marler & Tamura, *Science* 146:1483 (1964). Dialects are learned from older males in roughly the first 100 days of life.
- **Rapid dialect spread (Grounded).** Otter et al., "Continent-wide shifts in song dialects of white-throated sparrows", *Current Biology* (2020). A doublet-ending song from British Columbia replaced the traditional triplet ending across western Canada and reached western Quebec by 2019, ~3,300 km, likely via song tutoring on shared wintering grounds. The analysis used 1,785 recordings.
- **Vocal learning (Grounded).** Songbirds, parrots and hummingbirds are among the few animal groups that learn vocalisations, a model system for speech learning.
- **RGRF / Entanglements links (Speculative / creative):** bird song as a culturally transmitted, self-organising information network; UV and magnetic senses as "hidden-channel" perception for the novel.
