## 2. Tesla secondary winding
| Claim | Label | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tall slender helix on a non-conductive former, 4–6" × 20–40", 28–36 AWG, thousands of turns, single layer | Grounded | |
| "Tens of mH" | Grounded (range) | Rerun (Wheeler): 6" × 36" with 30 AWG close-wound ≈ 3,190 turns and ≈ 236 mH, so larger coils reach hundreds of mH |
| Self-resonance set by L, self-capacitance and topload | Grounded | The self-capacitance is **distributed** along the winding. The Medhurst estimate C ≈ H·D (pF, D in cm; H ≈ 0.5 at h/D ≈ 6) gives ≈ 7.6 pF for the example coil, so the bare coil self-resonates at ≈ 119 kHz (rerun). A topload lowers this |
| "Standing waves along the coil"; the "entire winding acts as coupled LC elements" | Grounded with correction | The secondary acts like a **quarter-wave helical resonator** (current maximum at the base, voltage maximum at the top). But the resonance is **not** where the wire is a quarter wavelength long. The 1,525 m of wire would naively give 49 kHz, but the actual resonance is ≈ 119 kHz, because propagation along a helix is not at c along the wire. Use lumped L + Medhurst C (plus topload), or a transmission-line model, not wire length. See [geometric_unity_review.md §3](geometric_unity_review.md#3-tesla-coil-physics-msg-6-checks-and-corrections) |
| Voltage step-up "via resonance with the primary" | Grounded | Ideal bound V2/V1 = √(L2/L1) = √(C1/C2) with energy transfer, k ≈ 0.1–0.2 for spark-gap coils. See [legal_safety_and_grounding_review.md §6](legal_safety_and_grounding_review.md#6-tesla-double-resonant-transformer-theory-message-8) |
| L ≈ μ₀N²A/l; f = 1/(2π√(LC)); Q = ωL/R | Grounded | The long-solenoid formula overestimates for h/D ≈ 3–6. Use Wheeler or Nagaoka corrections. Q must include radiation and dielectric losses, not just R_dc |
| Helices suffer "less optimal coupling to natural resonances like phi-based harmonics" | Symbolic / not supported | No "phi harmonic" exists in coil physics |
| Conical taper with base:top = φ; embedded golden spirals; "vortex-like energy flow"; 3-6-9 | Symbolic | Conical secondaries are real, but their benefit is tied to coupling and flashover, not to φ. Earlier RGRF coils used a measured spiral ratio of **1.335, not φ = 1.618** (#115). A φ taper is a valid design choice to test, but it carries no special resonance |
| Mycelium-hemp former: damping, "bio-resonance", "bio-plasma interactions" | Plausible-untested (former) / Speculative (rest) | Moist mycelium is not suitable for HV. Dried and sealed formers need dielectric-strength tests ([geometric_unity_review.md](geometric_unity_review.md)) |
| Reverse lay / forward lay "for cancelling parasitic effects" (msg 12) | [to verify] | "Lay" is a rope/cable-stranding term. For coils the established ideas are winding direction, bifilar and bank winding. Grok's definition is unsourced |

## 4. Light, refraction, Faraday and Verdet
| Claim | Label | Note |

| "Photons are repeatedly absorbed and re-emitted with tiny delays" | **Corrected** | This is a common misconception.
