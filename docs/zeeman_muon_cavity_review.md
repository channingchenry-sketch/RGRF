## 2. "Tungsten + muonic bismuth" coupled oscillators (messages 8, 16) — rerun

- **What Grok reported (message 8):** ω1 = 1, ω2 = 5.8 (5.0 + "B_effect" 0.8), k_c = 0.3, tungsten excited. "Clear oscillatory exchange and beating", with energy moving "from tungsten into the muonic-bismuth mode and back".
- **Rerun:** with those numbers, only ~1% of the energy ever appears in oscillator 2. That is a small, forced, off-resonance response; the normal-mode mixing is ~3 × 10^-4. Strong exchange needs nearly equal frequencies. So the reported "clear exchange" is **contradicted** by its own parameters.
- **Physics mapping:** a muonic atom's states are at keV–MeV energies on femtometre-to-picometre scales. A tungsten structure resonates at Hz–MHz. A shared spring constant between them has no physical meaning. **Symbolic.**
- **Message 16 stack sim** (mycelium base vs strained Bi-W layer, 15% strain shift, drive 0.2): ω1 was not given. With an assumed nearly-tuned pair, transfer is strong, as expected for any linear pair. Grok's conclusions about "electron-sheath and gamma interaction goals" do not follow from a mechanical oscillator model. Sim **Grok-reported**; interpretation **Symbolic**.
