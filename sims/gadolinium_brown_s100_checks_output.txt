== 1. Gadolinium magnetocaloric (Brown 1976: 14 K adiabatic, 4 kJ/kg isothermal at 7 T, Tc 293 K)
  mean adiabatic dT/dB over 0-7 T = 2.0 K/T (low-field slope ~2.5-3 K/T; effect sub-linear in B)
  dT_ad ~ Q/cp = 4000/236 = 17 K (upper bound; cp is larger near Tc, so 14 K is consistent)
  implied isothermal dS_M = 4000/293 = 13.7 J/(kg K)
  Brown 1976 regenerator span 46 C to -1 C = 47 K after ~50 cycles  (span > single-shot 14 K because of regeneration)
  Carnot COP (cooling) across that span = 5.8  (ceiling; no over-unity: COP>1 is heat moved per work, not energy created)
  permanent-magnet 1 T stage at ~2.5-3 K/T -> ~2.5-3 K single-shot lift (Grok: 'a few kelvin per stage' OK)
