A_2(n) = (-1)^n here, so Grok's 'phase that flips with n' is right.
Reading for n = 20: the HR leading formula gives 692.38 (gap +65), but the EXACT
Rademacher k = 1 term already gives ~625.8; the k = 2 term is only ~+1.3.
So the 65-count gap is mostly the crude asymptotic (dropping sinh->exp/2 and the
1/24 shift, and the 1/sqrt(n) correction), NOT the k = 2 arc. Grok's 'p2 is tens
of units' is too large for n = 20.
