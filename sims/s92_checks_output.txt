== 5. Anisotropic hoop term on a curved boundary, tangential b, radius R ==
div[(p_par-p_perp) bb] normal part = -(p_par-p_perp)/R (toward centre if p_par>p_perp)
=> p_par>p_perp ADDS inward pull (tension-like, firehose side); p_perp>p_par reduces it. Grok had it reversed.
