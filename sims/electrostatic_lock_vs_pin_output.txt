== Electrostatic pressure P = eps0*eps_r*E^2/2 ==
Air, cm-scale gap, ~3 MV/m breakdown                    E=3.000e+06 V/m  P=      39.84 Pa  (macro gap in air)
Air micro-gap ~7.5 um at Paschen minimum (~327 V)       E=4.360e+07 V/m  P=       8416 Pa  (best case for air gaps)
Polyimide film, 100 MV/m (ideal, no air gap)            E=1.000e+08 V/m  P=  1.505e+05 Pa  (upper bound, not reached in practice)
h-BN, 1.2 GV/m = 12 MV/cm (nm films, ideal)             E=1.200e+09 V/m  P=  2.231e+07 Pa  (Hattori 2015 breakdown; nm-thin only)

== Measured electroadhesion (for scale) ==
Diller, Majidi & Collins, ICRA 2016: one electrode pair bears up to 100 N,
engages/releases in <30 ms, leakage ~310 nA, ~0.6 mW while walking.
