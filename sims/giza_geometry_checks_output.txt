== Great Pyramid 'squaring the circle' ==
perimeter 4b = 921.3 m; 2*pi*h = 921.1 m; ratio = 1.0002
seked 5 1/2 palms -> slope 1.2727 (tan 51.84 deg); perimeter/height = 8/slope = 6.2857 = 2 x 3.1429 (22/7 = 3.1429)
-> the 2*pi match follows from the 5 1/2-palm seked; whether pi was intended is debated.
